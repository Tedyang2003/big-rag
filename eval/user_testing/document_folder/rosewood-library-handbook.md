# Rosewood Public Library — Staff Handbook

## About the Branch

The Rosewood branch opened on 14 March 1998 in the former telephone exchange
on Alder Street. It holds 84,000 items and serves roughly 2,300 registered
borrowers. The branch director is Marisol Okonkwo, appointed on 2 June 2021.

## Borrowing

Adult borrowers may hold 12 items at a time; children's cards allow 20. The
standard loan is 21 days, renewable twice unless another borrower has placed a
hold. Overdue items accrue a fine of 0.15 per day, capped at 12.00 per item.
Fines are waived entirely for borrowers under 16 and for anyone presenting a
current concession card.

## Interlibrary Loans

Requests go through the regional consortium and take 9 working days on average.
The consortium does not lend reference material, bound periodicals, or any item
published before 1920. A request costs 3.50, charged whether or not the item
arrives.

## Closures

The branch closes on 1 January, 1 May, 25 December and 26 December. It also
closed for 11 weeks from 7 September 2015 for roof repairs after water damage
destroyed 1,900 items in the local history collection.
