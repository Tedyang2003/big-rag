BANDED CIVET TRAIL CAMERA SURVEY
Thornwood Ridge Conservation Area
Survey window: 2 April 2025 to 28 September 2025

METHOD

Twelve passive infrared trail cameras were mounted at 1.4 metres on trees
along three transects. Cameras were set to a 15 second quiet period between
triggers and were checked every 21 days. All timestamps are local time.

RESULTS

Total triggers: 4,812
Triggers containing an identifiable animal: 1,106
Confirmed banded civet sightings: 47

Sightings by transect:
  North transect (cameras 1-4):   22
  Ridge transect (cameras 5-8):   19
  South transect (cameras 9-12):   6

Peak activity fell between 21:00 and 02:00. No civet was recorded in daylight
at any point in the survey.

EQUIPMENT NOTES

Camera 7 failed on 11 June 2025 after a battery seal leaked, and was not
replaced until 2 July 2025, leaving a 21 day gap on the ridge transect. Camera
3 was knocked out of alignment by cattle on 19 August 2025 but kept recording.

OTHER SPECIES RECORDED

Sambar deer (311 sightings), wild boar (204), leopard cat (88), pangolin (6),
and one sighting of a clouded leopard on 4 August 2025 — the first recorded in
this reserve since 2009.
