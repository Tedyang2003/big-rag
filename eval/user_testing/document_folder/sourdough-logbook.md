# Sourdough Logbook

## The Starter

The starter is called Bramble and was begun on 9 January 2024 from stoneground
rye and unchlorinated water. It is fed twice daily at a 1:2:2 ratio and kept at
21 degrees Celsius. Bramble doubles in 4 hours when healthy; anything slower
than 6 hours means it needs two days of twice-daily feeding before use.

## Standard Loaf

Final dough hydration is 78 percent. For a single 900 gram loaf:

- 400 g strong white flour
- 100 g wholemeal flour
- 390 g water
- 100 g active starter
- 10 g fine sea salt

## Method

Autolyse the flours and water for 45 minutes before adding starter and salt.
Bulk ferment for 4.5 hours at 24 degrees, with four sets of stretch and folds
in the first two hours. Pre-shape, rest 25 minutes, shape, then cold proof in
the refrigerator for between 12 and 18 hours.

Bake at 250 degrees with steam for 20 minutes, then at 220 degrees without
steam for a further 25 minutes. The loaf is done at an internal temperature of
96 degrees.

## Troubleshooting

A dense crumb almost always means under-fermentation rather than bad shaping.
A gummy centre means the loaf was cut before it had cooled for at least 2
hours. Large irregular holes near the crust point to over-proofing during the
cold rest.
