Thornley TPX-4420 Circulation Pump

Service manual, revision D, issued 18 February 2024. Supersedes revision C.

Specifications

  • Part number: TPX-4420
  • Impeller part number: TPX-4420-IMP-7
  • Rated flow: 240 litres per minute
  • Maximum head: 38 metres
  • Supply: 400 V three phase

Service Interval

The pump requires a full service every 1,800 running hours or every 18 months, whichever comes first. Seal inspection is required every 600 hours.

Torque Settings

Volute casing bolts are tightened to 42 Nm in a diagonal sequence. The impeller retaining nut takes 65 Nm. Do not exceed these values; the casing is cast aluminium and will crack before the bolt yields.

Seal Replacement

Isolate the supply and lock off before beginning. Drain the volute through the lower port, remove the eight casing bolts, and withdraw the rotating assembly. The mechanical seal is a cartridge type and must not be disassembled; replace it as a unit using part TPX-4420-SEAL-2.

Fault Codes

E04 indicates a dry run condition and locks the pump out for 10 minutes. E11 indicates a phase imbalance greater than 6 percent. E19 indicates bearing temperature above 95 degrees Celsius and requires the unit to be stopped immediately.
