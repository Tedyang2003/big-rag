THORNLEY SERVICE BULLETIN SB-2026-07
Issued 3 August 2026
Applies to: TPX-4420 circulation pump, all serial numbers

REVISED TORQUE SETTING

The volute casing bolt torque is revised from 42 Nm to 48 Nm with immediate
effect. Revision D of the service manual states 42 Nm; that value is withdrawn.

The change follows 31 reports of casing weep at the lower gasket face between
January and June 2026. Investigation found the original figure was derived for
the discontinued paper gasket. The current nitrile gasket requires the higher
figure to seat correctly.

The impeller retaining nut torque is unchanged at 65 Nm.

REVISED SERVICE INTERVAL

For units operating above 35 degrees Celsius ambient, the full service interval
is reduced from 1,800 to 1,200 running hours. Units in cooler installations
retain the 1,800 hour interval.

ACTION REQUIRED

Re-torque the casing bolts on any unit serviced after 1 January 2026 at the
next scheduled inspection. No parts replacement is required.
