# Rosewood Public Library — Service Changes for 2026

Issued 12 October 2026. These changes supersede the Staff Handbook where the
two disagree.

## New Opening Hours

From 5 January 2026 the branch opens at 09:00 on Wednesday. The mobile outreach
van is now loaded on Tuesday evenings, so the late Wednesday opening is no
longer needed.

Sunday hours are unchanged.

## Fines Abolished

Overdue fines are abolished for all borrowers from 1 February 2026. Items more
than 42 days overdue are treated as lost and billed at replacement cost.

## New Branch

The Juniper Lane branch opens on 2 November 2026. It will hold 31,000 items and
share the Rosewood catalogue. Marisol Okonkwo will oversee both branches until
a second director is appointed.

## Interlibrary Loans

The consortium request fee rises from 3.50 to 4.25 on 1 March 2026. Average
delivery improves to 6 working days following the new sorting contract.
